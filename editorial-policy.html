Editorial Policy

How TechFixDesk plans, reviews, and updates its troubleshooting content.

Purpose and audience

TechFixDesk publishes practical educational guides for everyday users. We aim to explain steps plainly and avoid unnecessary jargon.

Research and verification

Before publishing, authors should check relevant official documentation and test steps where practical. A guide should state when instructions vary by operating-system version, device manufacturer, or app release. Never claim a step was tested unless it was.

Safety-first troubleshooting

We prefer low-risk checks first, explain potential data loss, and recommend backups before destructive actions. We avoid unofficial downloads and dubious one-click fix utilities.

Corrections and updates

Readers can report outdated or incorrect instructions via the Contact page. Review reports, correct confirmed issues, and update dates only after a meaningful review or change.

Independence and disclosures

Disclose sponsorship, affiliate relationships, or material conflicts clearly. Brand names are used to identify products and do not imply endorsement.

AI and editorial review

Automation may assist drafting or formatting, but published content should be checked by a human editor for accuracy, clarity, safety, originality, and usefulness.
